Privacy Policy — Pulse

Pulse does not collect your data.

Last updated: September 24, 2026.

Local system statistics

Pulse reads CPU, GPU, memory, swap, disk and network usage statistics on your Mac to display graphs. Statistics remain in memory while the app is running and are not transmitted.

No data collection

Pulse has no accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or internet access. It does not read your documents, messages or browsing history and does not share data with third parties.

Settings and notifications

Settings are stored locally in your Mac’s preferences. Alerts are local macOS notifications. Exporting settings saves a file only to the location you select. Importing settings reads only the file you select.

Children

Pulse does not collect data from anyone, including children.
