Pulse — Mac Monitor

Pulse keeps your Mac’s CPU, GPU, memory and disk activity visible in the menu bar.

Your Mac at a glance

Read clear mini graphs and percentages, inspect every CPU core, and keep a floating dashboard above your windows or on your desktop.

Details in one click

CPU history and load averages, GPU usage, memory pressure and swap, available disk space, read/write activity and network rates.

Make it yours

Choose separate or combined menu bar items, colors, graph styles, refresh intervals and floating window layouts. Launch at login only if you choose to.

Private by design

No account, no data collection, no internet access, no advertising and no in-app purchases. Requires macOS 14 or later.
