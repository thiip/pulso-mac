Support — Pulse

Need help with Pulse?

Email suporte@vmsargus.com. Include your Mac model, macOS version and a description of the issue.

Where is the app?

Pulse runs in the menu bar at the top right of your screen and has no Dock icon. Click a menu bar item for details. On crowded menu bars, macOS can hide items behind the active app’s menus.

How do I open Settings?

Right-click any app item and choose Settings, or open the app again from Finder.

Why does CPU usage stop at 100%?

100% represents your entire Mac. Activity Monitor uses 100% per core.

Launch at login

Enable Launch at Login in the welcome window, quick menu or Settings. It is off until you choose to enable it.

Floating dashboard

Right-click a menu bar item and choose Floating Window. Right-click the dashboard to customize its layout and placement.

Uninstall

Disable Launch at Login, quit the app and remove it from Applications.
